# ClaimGate Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ClaimGate welcomes people who want to improve source-grounded review software, documentation, fixtures, tests, and domain packs. We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project space regardless of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and patient in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect different levels of familiarity with the codebase and domain.
- Disclose uncertainty and correct mistakes without blame.
- Protect private, sensitive, and personally identifiable information.
- Preserve ClaimGate's trust boundaries when discussing or proposing AI behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Knowingly submitting fabricated evidence, contributor history, benchmarks, or security claims.
- Repeated disruption after a maintainer has asked for a discussion to stop or move.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, project events, and public spaces where someone is representing ClaimGate.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/WooYoungSang). For a concern that is also a vulnerability, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer will review available evidence, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions will be based on the behavior and its impact, not on contributor status.

ClaimGate is currently maintained by one person, so response times are best-effort rather than guaranteed. If the report concerns the maintainer, reporters may use GitHub's platform-level reporting mechanisms instead of contacting the project directly.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1, and is adapted to ClaimGate's current solo-maintainer structure.
